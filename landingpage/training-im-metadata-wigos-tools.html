Capacity Development

Training on Meteorological Instrument Metadata and WIGOS Tools

12 – 16 October 2026 · 5 days · In-person training for DGMET Oman · Hosted by RWC Indonesia (BMKG), Jakarta

Date 12 – 16 October 2026
Duration 5 days
Location Calibration Laboratory, BMKG Headquarter Office, Stamet Soetta & Weather Radar Site
Participants Staff members from Oman (DGMET)
Speakers BMKG Team (DDK RWC Indonesia, DJK, DMP, PPSDM, DIK, Stamet Soetta)
Funding DGMET Oman

Training Objectives

  1. Strengthen understanding of WIGOS concept, operational workflows and related systems, including OSCAR/Surface, WDQMS, IMS, GBON, RBON and WIS 2.0.
  2. Enhance participants’ capacity in metadata management and WIGOS Station Identifier (WSI) implementation.
  3. Develop hands-on operational skills in the use of OSCAR/Surface, WDQMS, and IMS.
  4. Improve participants’ ability to analyze operational monitoring, data quality, and metadata issues.
  5. Promote operational collaboration, coordination, and experience sharing among participants.
  6. Enhance participants’ understanding of observation equipment operations, data processing, and practical applications through direct observation and technical discussions.

Expected Learning Outcomes

By the end of the training, participants are expected to be able to:

  1. Understand the concept and operational workflow of WIGOS and related systems.
  2. Manage, validate and apply WSI standards in OSCAR/Surface.
  3. Understand metadata governance and interoperability concepts within the WIGOS framework.
  4. Analyze data availability and quality issues using WDQMS.
  5. Use IMS for incident management and operational follow-up.
  6. Identify operational issues and recommend corrective actions.
