Yearly Report of RWC Indonesia 2025

RWC in Pilot Mode · Annual report of the Regional WIGOS Centre Indonesia for WMO Region V and WMO members supported by BMKG · Calendar year 2025

Reporting Period 1 January – 31 December 2025

I. Introduction

The Regional WIGOS Centre Indonesia operates as a single multifunctional RWC, with this report covering the activities of the whole RWC for calendar year 2025.

The systems used in RWC Indonesia activities during 2025 were:

  • WIGOS Data Quality Monitoring System (WDQMS)
  • Incident Management System (IMS)
  • OSCAR/Surface Metadata Management

This report is the result of monitoring from all Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) Centres available in WDQMS: DWD, ECMWF, JMA, and NCEP.

II. WDQMS-Related Functions

During 2025, RWC Indonesia monitored 218 stations spread across 8 countries: 110 stations in Indonesia (IDN), a station in Singapore (SGP), 33 stations in Malaysia (MYS), 50 stations in Philippines (PHL), a station in Brunei (BRN), 3 stations in Timor-Leste (TLS), 14 stations in Papua New Guinea (PNG), and 6 stations in the United States of America (USA). Monitoring was carried out on surface land and upper-air observation data from all countries, except Timor-Leste which does not send upper-air data. Surface land observations consist of 5 variables: surface pressure, 2m temperature, 10m zonal wind, 10m meridional wind, and relative humidity.

In daily monitoring, when an issue occurs at a station, a ticket is created in the IMS on the JIRA portal. However, if the station does not have a National Focal Point (NFP) — such as Timor-Leste and Papua New Guinea — the ticket is only recorded on Google Drive. During 2025 the tickets created covered surface land availability (Surf_A), surface land quality (Surf_Q), and upper-air availability (Upp_A). The number of issues per country and their current status in IMS is shown in Figure 2 of the original PDF. Details of tickets and issues are given in Table 1 and Table 2 below.

IV. Conclusion

  • Overall, there were 86 issues related to surface land observations, upper-air observations, and WIGOS metadata, broken down as follows:
    • Surface land observations: 65 issues
    • Upper-air observations: 19 issues
    • WIGOS metadata: 2 issues
  • Issues in Brunei and Papua New Guinea have not been recorded in IMS because the NFP is not yet available.
  • Several things still need to be developed, such as automation in the calculation of issues that have been created.
  • Regular meetings with the NFPs of all countries are still needed.
