About RWC Indonesia

Regional Collaboration for Integrated Observations

RWC Indonesia is operated by BMKG as part of the WMO Integrated Global Observing System (WIGOS) implementation. Together with WMO and RA-V member countries, we ensure weather, climate, and environmental observation data is available at the highest quality to support global meteorological services.

Our Services

RWC Indonesia provides technical services and coordination to strengthen the integrated observing system across the RA-V region

Metadata Management

Assisting member countries in managing observation station metadata on the OSCAR/Surface platform to ensure completeness and accuracy

Data Quality Monitoring

Monitoring data quality through WDQMS, identifying incidents, and coordinating with data providers for issue resolution

Regional Project Coordination

Coordinating and supporting the implementation of regional WIGOS projects to enhance observation coverage and efficiency

Capacity Development

Workshops, technical training, and mentoring for member countries on WIGOS standards implementation and data exchange
